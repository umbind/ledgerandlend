Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Essential Answers
EMI is calculated using the reducing balance compound formula: EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n – 1], where P is loan principal, r is monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100), and n is loan tenure in months.
The 50/30/20 rule divides post-tax income into: 50% Needs (housing, food, debt), 30% Wants (dining, hobbies), and 20% Savings & SIP Investments for long-term compound wealth creation.
Body Mass Index evaluates weight relative to height: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]².
• Underweight: < 18.5
• Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
• Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
• Obese: ≥ 30.0.
Naegele's rule estimates the Expected Date of Delivery (EDD) by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the Last Menstrual Period (LMP), assuming a standard 28-day cycle: EDD = LMP + 1 Year – 3 Months + 7 Days.
Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) measures how efficiently kidneys filter creatinine waste from blood using the race-free CKD-EPI 2021 equation. Normal eGFR is ≥ 90 mL/min/1.73m².
