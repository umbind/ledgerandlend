Frequently Asked Questions

Expert guidance, calculation formulas, and clear answers.

EMI uses the reducing balance formula: E = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1], where P is Principal, r is periodic interest rate, and n is total monthly tenure.
An amortization schedule details every payment over time, showing the exact portion going toward reducing the principal versus paying interest charges.
Making periodic prepayments toward the principal balance or opting for a shorter tenure dramatically decreases the compounding interest over the life of the loan.
